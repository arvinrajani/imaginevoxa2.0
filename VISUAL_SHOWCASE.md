### Transitions
- Duration: 200ms (fast), 300ms (normal), 500ms (slow)
- Easing: ease-in-out, cubic-bezier
- Properties: transform, opacity, colors

### Keyframes
- Spin: 360deg rotation
- Pulse: Scale 100% ↔ 105%
- Fade: Opacity 0 → 1
- Slide: Transform translateY
